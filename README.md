# Divine Connection

**Divine Connection** is a turn-based battle game where rival gods clash using the faith, devotion, and sacrifice of their followers.

Created for the GameDev.tv game jam, this prototype was built by 3 devs. We are beginner gamedevs, and pretty new to Unreal Engine. Our goal was to create a complete, playable experience within the time limit, and while the game still has bugs and rough edges, we are proud of what we managed to package, ship, and share.

We hope to keep improving Divine Connection after the jam and develop it into a fuller strategy game with deeper systems, stronger visuals, and more meaningful follower-based gameplay.

---

## The Idea

In Divine Connection, your followers are your power.

The stronger your connection with your followers, the more divine energy you can unleash. Every action matters: will you heal, attack, defend, sacrifice your own followers, convert the enemy’s believers, or save your power for a devastating miracle?

Victory is not just about dealing damage. It is about managing your followers, your connection, and your divine power better than your opponent.

---

## Core Gameplay

Divine Connection is built around simple turn-based decisions:

- **Gain strength from your followers**
- **Build connection to unlock stronger divine actions**
- **Attack or defend against the enemy god**
- **Convert enemy followers to weaken your opponent**
- **Sacrifice followers for short-term power**
- **Unleash a Miracle when the time is right**

The prototype currently focuses on a one-on-one god battle system, but our original vision included visible followers spawning into the arena and fighting on behalf of their gods.

---

## Abilities

### Pray  
Heal yourself through the devotion of your followers.

### Smite  
Strike the enemy god with divine force.

### Sacrifice  
Give up some of your followers to gain connection.

### Convert  
Turn enemy followers to your side.

### Defend  
Reduce incoming damage until your next turn.

### Miracle  
Unleash your ultimate ability, using the power of your followers to heavily damage the enemy.

## Current Prototype Notes

This is a game jam prototype, so you may encounter bugs, balance issues, missing polish, and general weirdness.

Some known limitations:

- The game balance is still experimental.
- Some abilities may feel stronger or weaker than intended.
- Visual feedback is limited in some areas.
- The follower system is currently mostly represented through stats rather than fully animated characters.
- UI, menus, and game flow are still early prototype quality.

Even with those limitations, the game is fully playable and represents the foundation of the idea we wanted to explore.

---

## Future Plans

There were many features we wanted to include but could not finish within the jam deadline.

Some ideas we would love to explore in the future:

- Visible followers spawning in the arena
- Followers fighting on behalf of their gods
- Better ability animations and visual effects
- Improved UI feedback for gains, losses, and ability costs
- More strategic ability balancing
- Multiple enemy gods with different behaviours
- More arenas and levels
- Main menu, pause menu, and better win/loss screens
- Stronger connection mechanics tied directly to followers
- A more polished tutorial/introduction

Our long-term goal would be to turn Divine Connection into a fuller turn-based god battle game where follower management is the heart of the strategy.
